## Why Asking Questions Matters

| What Interviewers Assess | What Your Questions Signal |
|--------------------------|---------------------------|
| Genuine interest | You researched the role and company |
| Technical maturity | You think about production realities |
| Team-fit | You care about collaboration and culture |
| Long-term thinking | You're evaluating mutual fit, not just accepting |
| SRE mindset | You ask about SLOs, on-call, toil — not just tech stack |

**Rule of thumb:** Prepare 8-10 questions; ask 3-5 based on time and conversation flow.

## Questions About the Team

These show you care about the people and collaboration dynamics.

### Recommended Questions

1. **"Can you tell me more about the team I'd be working with — size, structure, and how it fits within the broader technology organization?"**

2. **"How does the SRE team collaborate with development, QA, and security teams day to day?"**

3. **"What does the on-call rotation look like — frequency, hours, and how is it shared across the team?"**

4. **"Is the team distributed across locations/time zones? How do you handle collaboration?"**

5. **"What does a typical team meeting or sprint cadence look like?"**

### Why These Work

- Shows interest in **collaboration** (HSBC value: We succeed together)
- Practical — you want to understand your daily environment
- On-call question shows you've thought about the reality of SRE work

## Questions About the Role

From HSBC preparation guidelines — these were explicitly suggested.

### Recommended Questions

1. **"What is the main focus of the project or team right now?"**

2. **"What would be expected of me in the first few months?"**

3. **"What does a typical workday look like in this role?"**

4. **"What are the biggest challenges the team is facing currently?"**

5. **"How is success measured for this role in the first 6-12 months?"**

6. **"What happened to the previous person in this role — is this a new position or a backfill?"**

### Why These Work

- Directly from HSBC interview preparation recommendations
- Shows you're planning how to succeed, not just land the job
- "Biggest challenges" invites honest conversation and shows confidence

## Questions About the Technical Environment

Demonstrate technical curiosity and production awareness.

### Recommended Questions

1. **"What does the cloud and Kubernetes infrastructure look like — which cloud providers, cluster size, GitOps tooling?"**

2. **"What monitoring and observability stack does the team use? Are SLOs formally defined?"**

3. **"How are CI/CD pipelines structured — what's the path from code commit to production?"**

4. **"What's the deployment frequency and what deployment strategies are used — rolling, canary, blue-green?"**

5. **"How does the team handle secrets management and compliance requirements in the pipeline?"**

6. **"Is there a service mesh in place (Istio)? What problem did it solve?"**

7. **"What's the current state of infrastructure as code — Terraform, Helm, Kustomize?"**

### Why These Work

- Directly maps to job description requirements
- Shows you think about **production systems**, not just development
- Invites the interviewer to talk about their work (people enjoy this)

## Questions About SRE Practices

Show SRE maturity — these differentiate you from generic DevOps candidates.

### Recommended Questions

1. **"How does the team define and track SLOs? Is there an error budget policy?"**

2. **"What does the incident response process look like — severity levels, incident commander, postmortem culture?"**

3. **"How much toil does the team currently handle, and what's being done to reduce it?"**

4. **"Are there regular game days or chaos engineering exercises?"**

5. **"How are runbooks maintained — is there a documentation culture?"**

6. **"What's the alert noise level like — pages per week on average?"**

7. **"How does the team balance reliability work against feature development?"**

### Why These Work

- Demonstrates **SRE-specific thinking** (not just "I know Kubernetes")
- Shows you've read the Google SRE book or equivalent
- Practical questions that reveal team maturity

## Questions About Culture & Growth

Show long-term interest and alignment with HSBC values.

### Recommended Questions

1. **"How would you describe the team culture — and how does it reflect HSBC's values?"**

2. **"What opportunities are there for learning and professional development — conferences, certifications, internal training?"**

3. **"How does HSBC support career progression for SREs — technical track vs management?"**

4. **"What's something you personally enjoy about working on this team at HSBC?"**

5. **"How does the team approach knowledge sharing — tech talks, documentation, pairing?"**

6. **"What does HSBC's 'How We Lead' framework mean in practice for technology teams?"**

### Why These Work

- Shows you're evaluating **mutual fit**, not desperate
- References HSBC-specific initiatives (How We Lead, values)
- Personal question (#4) builds rapport

## Questions Aligned with HSBC Values

Map questions to HSBC's four values for maximum impact.

| HSBC Value | Question |
|------------|----------|
| **We get it done** | "What's a recent example where the team moved quickly to solve a critical problem?" |
| **We value difference** | "How does the team ensure diverse perspectives in technical decisions and postmortems?" |
| **We take responsibility** | "How does the team handle accountability during incidents — is it truly blameless?" |
| **We succeed together** | "Can you give an example of a cross-team initiative that improved reliability?" |

### HSBC-Specific Questions

1. **"HSBC's ambition is to be the most trusted bank globally — how does the SRE team's work directly support that trust?"**

2. **"I noticed HSBC is investing heavily in AI and digital transformation — how is that affecting the infrastructure and platform teams?"**

3. **"How does HSBC's global presence affect infrastructure decisions — data residency, multi-region deployments?"**

4. **"What role does sustainability play in infrastructure decisions — cloud efficiency, carbon-aware scheduling?"**

## Questions to Avoid

| Avoid | Why | Better Alternative |
|-------|-----|-------------------|
| "What does HSBC do?" | Shows zero preparation | "I read about HSBC's four business segments..." |
| "How much vacation do I get?" | HR question, not interviewer | Ask HR/recruiter separately |
| "What's the salary?" | Too early unless they bring it up | "Can you share the compensation range for this role?" (to recruiter) |
| "Will I have to work weekends?" | Sounds unwilling | "What does the on-call rotation look like?" |
| "Can I work fully remote?" | May exist but frame better | "What's the team's approach to hybrid working?" |
| "How quickly can I get promoted?" | Sounds impatient | "What does career progression look like for SREs?" |
| No questions at all | Missed opportunity | Always ask at least 2-3 |

## Question Selection Strategy

### By Interview Stage

| Stage | Interviewer | Best Questions |
|-------|-------------|----------------|
| **Recruiter screen** | HR/Recruiter | Role scope, team size, process, timeline |
| **Technical interview** | Engineer/Lead | Tech stack, SLOs, on-call, deployment process |
| **Hiring manager** | Manager | Team challenges, expectations, growth, culture |
| **Final panel** | Senior leadership | Strategy, HSBC values, organizational priorities |

### By Time Available

| Time | Ask |
|------|-----|
| **5 minutes** | 2-3 focused questions |
| **10 minutes** | 4-5 questions across categories |
| **15+ minutes** | Follow-up questions based on conversation |

### HSBC Preparation Guideline Examples

Your preparation document specifically suggested:

1. ✅ "Can you tell me more about the team I'd be working with?"
2. ✅ "What is the main focus of the project right now?"
3. ✅ "What would be expected of me in the first few months?"
4. ✅ "What does a typical workday look like in this role?"
5. ✅ "When can I expect to hear back with feedback after the interview?"

### Closing Question (Always Ask One)

> **"When can I expect to hear back with feedback after the interview?"**

Or:

> **"Is there anything about my background or answers today that I can clarify?"**

This shows confidence and gives you a chance to address any concerns.
