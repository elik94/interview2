🔹 4. STAR Method (for competency-based questions)
Familiarize yourself with the STAR technique

Useful source:
📘 STAR Interview Guide
When you’ll be answering technical questions, it’s helpful to use a structured method:

Situation – Describe the event or situation that you were in.
Task – Explain the task you had to complete.
Action – Describe the specific actions you took to complete the task. (focus on “I”, not “we”)
Result – Close with the result of your efforts.

Prepare for 3 types of answers:
1.	A commercial example
2.	A non-commercial but relevant example
3.	If no example – how you would act in such a situation

🔹 6. End of Interview – Your Questions
